# Maple Court Property Management - Maintenance Policy (fictional)

## P-1 Emergency: safety and major damage
Urgency: emergency
Category: safety
Keywords: gas, smell, fire, smoke, flood, flooding, burst, sparking, electrical, carbon, monoxide, sewage, ceiling collapse
Rule: Risk to life or major property damage. Dispatch on-call technician immediately (target response 2 hours). Tenant should be told to leave the area if gas, smoke or fire is involved.

## P-2 Urgent: loss of essential service
Urgency: urgent
Category: essential-service
Keywords: heating, heater, boiler, hot water, no water, toilet, lock, locked, broken lock, fridge, refrigerator, stove
Rule: An essential service is unavailable (heat, hot water, running water, working toilet, secure door). Schedule within 24 hours.

## P-3 Routine: leaks and minor plumbing
Urgency: routine
Category: plumbing
Keywords: leak, leaking, tap, faucet, drip, dripping, drain, clogged, blocked, sink
Rule: Contained leaks and slow drains with no active flooding. Schedule within 3 business days. Upgrade to P-1 if water is spreading or near electrics.

## P-4 Routine: appliances and fixtures
Urgency: routine
Category: appliance-fixture
Keywords: dishwasher, oven, washer, dryer, light, bulb, window, blinds, cabinet, door, handle
Rule: Non-essential appliance or fixture faults. Schedule within 5 business days.

## P-5 Low: cosmetic and requests
Urgency: low
Category: cosmetic
Keywords: paint, scuff, scratch, carpet, stain, squeak, wall, garden, hedge
Rule: Cosmetic issues. Batch into the next monthly maintenance round.

## P-6 General: unclassified
Urgency: routine
Category: general
Keywords:
Rule: Fallback when nothing else matches. A human must confirm the category and urgency before approval.
